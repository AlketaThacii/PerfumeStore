Shipping Policy

Shipping Terms

1. General Information

This Shipping Policy explains how orders are processed, shipped, and delivered through our online store. By placing an order on our website, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

2. Order Processing

All orders are processed within a standard processing timeframe after payment confirmation. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays may be processed on the next business day.

3. Shipping Methods

We use trusted courier and delivery services to ensure that your order is delivered safely and efficiently. Shipping methods may vary depending on your location and order size.

4. Delivery Timeframes

Estimated delivery times are provided for convenience only and may vary due to factors beyond our control, including courier delays, weather conditions, or high-volume periods. We do not guarantee exact delivery dates.

5. Shipping Costs

Shipping fees are calculated at checkout based on the delivery address, selected shipping method, and order value. Any applicable shipping charges will be clearly displayed before order confirmation.

6. Order Tracking

Where available, tracking information will be provided once your order has been dispatched. Tracking availability depends on the courier service used.

7. International Shipping

International shipping may be available to selected locations. Customers are responsible for any customs duties, taxes, or import fees imposed by local authorities.

8. Delivery Issues

If an order is delayed, lost, or delivered incorrectly, please contact us as soon as possible. We will work with the courier service to investigate and provide an appropriate solution.

9. Incorrect Address

Customers are responsible for providing accurate and complete shipping information. We are not responsible for delivery issues resulting from incorrect or incomplete addresses provided at checkout.

10. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns regarding shipping or delivery, please contact us through the communication channels available on our website. Our support team will assist you promptly.
