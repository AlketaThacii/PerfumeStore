Refund Policy

Refund Terms

1. General Information

This Refund Policy outlines the conditions under which refunds may be granted for products purchased through our online store. By placing an order on our website, you acknowledge and agree to the terms described in this policy.

2. Eligibility for Refunds

Due to hygiene and safety considerations associated with perfumes and fragrance products, refunds are only available for items that are unused, unopened, and returned in their original packaging. Products that show signs of use, damage, or tampering are not eligible for a refund.

3. Refund Requests

All refund requests must be submitted within the specified return period from the date of delivery. Requests made after this period may not be accepted. Proof of purchase may be required to process a refund request.

4. Inspection & Approval

Once a returned product is received, it will be inspected to ensure it meets the refund eligibility criteria. Refunds will only be approved after successful inspection. We reserve the right to refuse a refund if the returned item does not comply with this policy.

5. Refund Method & Processing Time

Approved refunds will be issued using the original payment method. Processing times may vary depending on the payment provider or financial institution. Please allow a reasonable period for the refunded amount to reflect in your account.

6. Non-Refundable Items

Certain items may be marked as non-refundable at the time of purchase. These items are not eligible for refunds under any circumstances unless required by applicable consumer protection laws.

7. Shipping Costs

Shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is a result of an error on our part, such as receiving an incorrect or defective product. Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer unless otherwise stated.

8. Damaged or Incorrect Products

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, please contact us promptly with supporting evidence. Eligible cases will be reviewed, and an appropriate resolution will be provided in accordance with this policy.

9. Policy Updates

We reserve the right to update, modify, or replace this Refund Policy at any time without prior notice. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting on the website. It is your responsibility to review this policy periodically.

10. Contact

For any questions or concerns regarding refunds, please contact us through the communication channels provided on our website. Our support team will assist you as promptly as possible.
